# Estimation & Launch Readiness

## ETA philosophy
Never claim false precision. Return optimistic, most-likely and contingency ranges plus confidence, assumptions, critical path and unknowns.

As history develops, recalibrate from actual velocity, failed attempts, review/rework rate, test failure rate, integration blockers and human response delay. Anonymized aggregate calibration is optional only with appropriate privacy/legal design.

## Readiness categories
Functionality, security, privacy/data, billing, observability, recovery, support, legal and distribution.

Each control has required/optional status, evidence state, evidence IDs, owner/assignee and freshness. A required unresolved control blocks readiness unless the framework explicitly permits and records owner risk acceptance.

## Score use
A percentage may summarize coverage but never override blocking controls. Always show the verdict and exact blockers.
