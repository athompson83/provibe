# Legal & Launch

## Required customer-facing documents before paid beta
Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie/analytics disclosure as applicable, Acceptable Use Policy, subprocessors/data-processing disclosures, security overview, support/refund policy, and provider/integration disclosures.

## Key contractual/product disclosures
- Product provides technical information and AI-assisted interpretation, not a guarantee of security or production readiness.
- Evidence states and limitations are visible.
- Customer represents they have authority to connect repositories/accounts.
- Source handling/retention/deletion are disclosed.
- Third-party provider availability/scopes are external dependencies.

## Operational launch checklist
Domain/brand search, billing/tax handling, privacy review, incident contact, backups/recovery, deletion/export test, integration disconnect/revocation test, error monitoring, support channel, rate/usage limits, abuse controls and status communication.

## Beta gate
Functionality, security, privacy/data handling, billing, observability, recovery, support, legal and distribution controls must each have explicit evidence or accepted-risk decisions. No single opaque score overrides a missing critical gate.
