- [ ] Functional critical paths verified.
- [ ] Security/authorization negative tests pass.
- [ ] Privacy/data handling reviewed.
- [ ] Billing tested where applicable.
- [ ] Monitoring/alerts active.
- [ ] Backup/recovery/rollback proven.
- [ ] Support ownership defined.
- [ ] Legal pages/disclosures published.
- [ ] Analytics/distribution ready.
- [ ] Required risks explicitly accepted or resolved.
